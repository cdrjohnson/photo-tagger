# Sample image attribution

Sample photos used by the end-to-end tests and screenshots. All were downloaded from Wikimedia Commons (1024 px wide thumbnails) and are CC0 or public domain. The five White House / FEMA photos (biden-*, obama-*) are works of the US federal government (public domain in the US) and are used to test face recognition: the same person (Joe Biden) in four photos – one of them together with Barack Obama – plus a portrait of Barack Obama as a distractor. person.jpg (Kalpana Chawla, NASA) is a second distractor.

## Generated variants (for duplicate-detection tests)

Derived from the public-domain `dog.jpg` above; no new third-party content.

- `dog-renamed.jpg` – byte-identical copy under a different file name
- `dog-recompress.jpg` – resized to 900 px wide, JPEG quality 55
- `dog-crop.jpg` – 10 px crop on each side, JPEG quality 85
- `dropbox-stub.jpg` – synthetic high-frequency noise JPEG (Dropbox Chooser stub in tests; deliberately unlike other samples so it is not a near-duplicate)
- `dropbox-import-a.jpg` / `dropbox-import-b.jpg` – synthetic noise JPEGs for the Dropbox Chooser stub (unique so they are not near-duplicates of other samples)
